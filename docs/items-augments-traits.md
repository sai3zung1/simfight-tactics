# Items, augments and traits

Three ways to put effects on a board. What they carry is identical; only the gate
differs.

Each is a named, described, icon-bearing list of modifiers, and everything in
that list is drawn from one vocabulary — `docs/effect-families.md`.

| Family  | What opens the gate                     | What it adds to the shape                                |
| ------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Item    | Equipped on a unit, three slots at most | A type: component, craftable, radiant, artifact, emblem  |
| Augment | Picked for the run, three slots at most | A tier: silver, gold or prismatic                        |
| Trait   | A unit count reaching a breakpoint      | Breakpoints, each with its own count and its own effects |

A board side names a unit, its star level, its items, its traits and its
augments.
